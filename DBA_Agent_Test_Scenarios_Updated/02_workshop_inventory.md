# Test scenario 02 --- Neighborhood repair workshop inventory

> **Use only Section 1 to start the agent.** Keep Sections 2--4 as the
> interviewer answer sheet. Do not give Section 5 to the agent.

## 1. Opening description

A family-owned electronics repair workshop needs a local system for
customers, parts inventory, repair jobs, invoices, and payments. The
owner wants it to work during internet outages. Two staff members take
turns using one counter computer. The workshop has no IT staff and does
not want to install or maintain a separate database server.

## 2. Canonical interview answers

The facts below are definitive. They resolve the schema, transaction,
accounting, workload, security, and deployment questions.

### 2.1 Entities, attributes, and data rules

IDs are signed 64-bit integer surrogate keys generated by the database.
All timestamps are UTC. The workshop uses one currency only: GBP. Store
all money as integer pence; do not use floating-point values.

-   **Customer:** `customer_id` PK; `display_name` VARCHAR(120)
    required; `phone` VARCHAR(30) nullable; `email` VARCHAR(254)
    nullable. Email and phone are not unique because family members may
    share contact details.
-   **Staff:** `staff_id` PK; `username` VARCHAR(64) required and
    unique; `display_name` VARCHAR(120) required; `role` restricted to
    `owner` or `technician`; `active` BOOLEAN required.
-   **Part:** `part_id` PK; `sku` VARCHAR(64) required and unique;
    `name` VARCHAR(160) required; `unit_cost_pence` BIGINT required and
    \>= 0; `reorder_level` INTEGER required and \>= 0; `active` BOOLEAN
    required.
-   **RepairJob:** `job_id` PK; `ticket_number` VARCHAR(32) required and
    unique; `customer_id` FK required; `opened_by_staff_id` FK required;
    `assigned_staff_id` FK nullable; `status` restricted to `received`,
    `diagnosing`, `awaiting_customer`, `in_progress`, `awaiting_parts`,
    `ready_for_collection`, `collected`, `cancelled`; `opened_at_utc`
    required; `closed_at_utc` nullable. `collected` and `cancelled`
    require `closed_at_utc`.
-   **JobPart:** `job_part_id` PK; `job_id` FK required; `part_id` FK
    required; `quantity` INTEGER required and \> 0; `unit_price_pence`
    BIGINT required and \>= 0, preserving the price quoted when the part
    was used. Unique `(job_id, part_id)`; a part is represented once per
    job and quantity is aggregated.
-   **StockMovement:** `movement_id` PK; `part_id` FK required;
    `quantity_delta` INTEGER required and nonzero; `reason` restricted
    to `opening_balance`, `purchase_receipt`, `job_consumption`,
    `customer_return`, or `manual_adjustment`; `occurred_at_utc`
    required; `job_part_id` FK nullable and unique. For
    `job_consumption`, `job_part_id` is required and `quantity_delta`
    equals the negative JobPart quantity. For all other reasons,
    `job_part_id` is null. Current stock is the sum of all movement
    deltas for a part.
-   **Invoice:** `invoice_id` PK; `invoice_number` VARCHAR(32) required
    and unique; `job_id` FK required and unique (at most one invoice per
    job); `issued_at_utc` required; `total_pence` BIGINT required and
    \>= 0; `status` restricted to `issued` or `void`. An invoice may be
    voided only before any payment is recorded. The total is the
    owner-approved final amount for parts and labour; there is no
    separate tax calculation or invoice-line table in this version.
-   **Payment:** `payment_id` PK; `invoice_id` FK required;
    `amount_pence` BIGINT required and \> 0; `method` restricted to
    `cash`, `card_terminal`, or `bank_transfer`; `paid_at_utc` required;
    optional `receipt_reference` VARCHAR(80). Receipt reference is
    unique when present.

### 2.2 Relationships and cardinalities

-   A Customer has zero or more RepairJobs; each RepairJob has exactly
    one Customer.
-   A Staff member may open or be assigned many jobs. A job has one
    required opener and at most one current assignee.
-   A RepairJob has zero or more JobParts and at most one Invoice.
-   A Part has many StockMovements and may appear in many JobParts.
-   A JobPart has exactly one corresponding `job_consumption`
    StockMovement. The movement is linked by unique `job_part_id`.
-   An Invoice has one or more Payments; a payment belongs to exactly
    one invoice.
-   Historical invoices, payments, jobs, and movements are retained.
    Staff are disabled, not deleted.
-   Parent deletion is restricted when history exists. No cascade
    deletion of invoices, payments, jobs, or stock movements.

### 2.3 Data volume and retention

-   4,000 active/inactive SKUs, 10,000 customers, 8,000 historical jobs,
    50,000 stock movements, and 12,000 invoices at initial migration.
-   Add about 2,000 jobs and 15,000 stock movements per year.
-   Retain invoices, payments, jobs, and stock movements for seven
    years; retain customer contact data only as long as operationally
    required, then anonymize it while preserving financial history.
-   Expected database size is below 1 GB for seven years. No images or
    repair documents are stored in the database.

### 2.4 Workload and reporting

-   One Windows 11 counter PC with a local SSD. Two staff accounts take
    turns; only one application process and one writer are active at a
    time.
-   Approximately 80% reads and 20% short writes.
-   Common reads: ticket/SKU lookup, open-job queue, part stock balance,
    weekly revenue, and reorder list.
-   Weekly revenue is grouped by payment date and invoice; no analytics
    warehouse, public website, remote client, or multi-location
    reporting is required.
-   Normal counter searches should finish within two seconds; weekly
    reports within 15 seconds.

### 2.5 Transactions and consistency

-   Adding a JobPart, adding its linked negative StockMovement, and
    updating the job are one atomic transaction.
-   Before a job-consumption transaction commits, the resulting stock
    balance must remain \>= 0. Check the aggregate balance inside the
    same transaction; a simple row-level CHECK on `quantity_delta` is
    not sufficient.
-   Receiving parts creates positive StockMovements. Opening stock is
    represented by `opening_balance` movements.
-   Issuing an Invoice and recording its initial status is atomic.
-   A Payment and its receipt reference are recorded atomically. The sum
    of non-void Payments for an Invoice must never exceed
    `Invoice.total_pence`; enforce this in transactional application
    logic or an equivalent safe database mechanism.
-   A failed operation leaves no partial JobPart, StockMovement,
    Invoice, or Payment.
-   No simultaneous writers, distributed transactions, or background
    write-heavy jobs.

### 2.6 Security and privacy

-   Application roles are `owner` and `technician`. The owner alone can
    view weekly financial reports, void invoices, and issue adjustments.
    Technicians can manage jobs and stock but cannot view aggregate
    financial reports.
-   Role checks are enforced by the application. OS account permissions
    and full-disk encryption protect the local database file.
-   No database login, remote access, customer portal, or network file
    share is required.
-   No passwords or payment-card numbers are stored. Card payments are
    recorded only as method and amount; processing occurs on a separate
    terminal.

### 2.7 Business rules

-   SKU, ticket number, invoice number, and non-null receipt reference
    are unique.
-   Part costs and invoice totals are nonnegative; payment amounts are
    positive; all money is integer pence.
-   Stock movements are signed; zero movement is prohibited. Current
    stock may never become negative.
-   Every JobPart has exactly one matching negative movement; a
    non-consumption movement cannot reference a JobPart.
-   A job can have at most one invoice. A payment total cannot exceed
    the invoice total.
-   `collected` and `cancelled` jobs require a close timestamp.
-   Valid status/reason/method values are exactly those listed above.
-   No VAT, multi-currency, refunds, customer self-service, barcode
    scanner integration, or multi-branch stock transfer is required. If
    a payment must be corrected, the owner records an audited manual
    adjustment outside this schema; the system does not issue refunds.

### 2.8 Deployment and operations

-   One Windows 11 workstation, local SSD, standalone installer, fully
    usable during internet outages.
-   No database server, Docker, shell access, remote database, network
    share, or administrator installation privileges.
-   SQLite 3.42 or later is bundled with the application runtime.
-   Daily backup after closing. RPO is 24 hours; RTO is two hours.
    Backups are encrypted and tested monthly.
-   No future multi-location expansion is part of this test.

## 3. Exact answers to likely follow-up questions

-   **Currency?** GBP only; integer pence; no tax or currency
    conversion.
-   **Can a customer email be shared?** Yes. Email is optional and not
    unique.
-   **How is stock calculated?** Sum `quantity_delta` across
    StockMovement rows for the part.
-   **Can stock go negative?** No. The application must check the
    aggregate balance transactionally before commit.
-   **Can an invoice have multiple payments?** Yes, including partial
    payments; their non-void total cannot exceed invoice total.
-   **Are invoice lines required?** No. Invoice total is the
    owner-approved final amount; no invoice-line entity in this version.
-   **Are there multiple writers?** No. Staff take turns at one PC.
-   **Anything not listed?** It is out of scope; do not invent a
    requirement.

## 5. Expected outcome --- evaluator only

**Do not send this section to the agent.**

-   **Expected `selected`:** `SQLite`
-   **Expectation strength:** Strong.

### Why

A single local Windows workstation, one writer, offline operation, no
server installation, and modest data size favor an embedded file
database. The aggregate stock and payment invariants require
transactional application logic but do not require a server DBMS.

### Design and SQL acceptance checks

-   Unique SKU, ticket number, invoice number, and optional receipt
    reference.
-   `JobPart` and its exactly matching negative `StockMovement` must be
    committed together.
-   Current stock is the sum of movements and must never be negative.
-   Payment sum cannot exceed invoice total; do not pretend a simple row
    CHECK can enforce this cross-row rule.
-   Seed negatives: duplicate SKU, negative part cost, zero payment,
    orphan invoice, invalid job status, consumption without a matching
    movement, movement whose quantity does not equal
    `-JobPart.quantity`, and overpayment.
-   Do not add a separate server or multi-location features.
